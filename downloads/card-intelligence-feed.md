# Card Intelligence Feed

Updated: 2026-07-08T09:45:00.000Z

Boundary: this feed provides research routing, scoring context, update triggers and source-ledger pointers. It is not an accusation system and does not assert guilt, intent, hidden control or unlawful conduct.

## Scoring Model

Scores rank research relevance and update priority. They do not prove claims.

Dimensions:

- publicRole
- networkReach
- updatePriority
- evidenceRoute
- narrativeSignal
- legalProximity

## Source Rule

No source link, no ledger entry.
