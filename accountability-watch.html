Accountability Watch

ACCOUNTABILITY WATCH.

This page tracks the records that matter most for public accountability: courts, regulators, procurement, sanctions, filings, lobbying, disclosures and missing documents.

Evidence boundary: Accountability watch items require public records. Leads and allegations stay as leads until records are attached.

Strong record types

  • Court dockets, judgments and exhibits
  • Regulator notices, fines, enforcement actions and settlements
  • Sanctions listings and official designations
  • Procurement files, contracts and disputes
  • Lobbying filings and meeting calendars
  • Company registers, beneficial ownership records and annual reports
  • Foundation filings, grants and board records

Conclusion standard

Every accountability conclusion must show: source, date, what the record proves, what it suggests, what it does not prove, confidence level, missing record and next action.
