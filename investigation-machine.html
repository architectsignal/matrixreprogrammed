Non-Negotiable Evidence Rules

  • Primary government, court, regulator, inspector-general and official archive records receive the highest evidence weight.
  • A conviction, judgment, guilty plea, official sanction or final enforcement order may support a finding of established wrongdoing within the exact scope of that record.
  • An indictment, charge, complaint, investigation or allegation is not proof of guilt and must be labelled accordingly.
  • Leaks and archive releases are evidence leads. Documents must be authenticated, dated, contextualised and cross-checked before they support a substantive accusation.
  • Every conclusion must state the source, evidence grade, what the record establishes, what it does not establish, the mechanism, affected power route and the next record needed.
  • Source failures, removed files, changed indexes, unexplained gaps and contradictory versions are tracked as missing-record or transparency findings, not automatic proof of corruption.
  • The machine investigates vigorously but does not invent intent, guilt, coordination or criminality beyond the evidence.

Evidence rule: convictions and final official outcomes may establish wrongdoing within their exact scope. Charges are allegations. Leaks are leads until authenticated and corroborated.
