Deep Review

SPECULATION REVIEW.

This section goes deep without turning unsupported material into fact. Each claim becomes a record route, counter-source map and probability test.

Speculation LabOutcome BriefingsSource Vault

Evidence Rule

Unverified claims stay labelled as claims. The page asks what record would prove, weaken or falsify the claim.

Case File Method

STEP 1

Write the exact claim.

No vague fog. The claim must be specific enough to test.

STEP 2

Find the original source chain.

Archive page, document, video, metadata, date, first known appearance and copied versions.

STEP 3

Separate record from interpretation.

A real email, file or photo can exist while a viral interpretation of it remains unsupported.

STEP 4

List counter-sources.

The page is stronger when it shows what weakens the claim.

STEP 5

Define probability triggers.

What new record would raise or lower the probability?

STEP 6

Publish outcome, not certainty.

The result should be: unsupported, weak lead, live lead, documented association, or record-backed finding.

Priority Review Cases

CASE REVIEW

Clinton-email archive claim route

Status: claim-review route, not a verified finding.

Useful work: search official email archive references, terms, attachments, metadata and dates; separate real archive material from viral interpretation.

Records needed: original archived item, chain of appearance, official archive result, counter-source, metadata and independent verification.

Outcome rule: no page upgrades unless a specific archived record supports the exact claim.

CASE REVIEW

Coded-language claim route

Status: interpretation watch.

Useful work: distinguish ordinary language, slang, private shorthand, proven coded language and unsupported decoding.

Records needed: same-term use across records, external corroboration, court record or investigative source.

CASE REVIEW

Elite-access network route

Status: association map.

Useful work: separate contact, meeting, travel, funding, employment, legal record and direct evidence.

Records needed: logs, filings, court exhibits, direct statements and counter-records.

Figures & Sources

Figures on this page

Checked: 1 July 2026

Numbers on this page are public-record leads. Crime, migration, sexual-offence, payout, death, and crisis figures must be tied to named official, court, police, parliamentary, regulator, or reputable source records before they are presented as settled.

Evidence standard

What counts as a usable figure?

Official statistics, court records, ministry releases, police datasets, parliamentary material, regulator data, or clearly named public reports. Nationality, foreign-born status, asylum status, immigration status, charge, suspect, conviction, and victim categories must not be mixed.

Document trail

Open the underlying record

When the figure matters, follow the document link, source file, court record, official release, PDF, book, or video. The visible page should help readers reach the underlying evidence fast.
