# Black File Exposé Engine

Updated: 2026-07-02

Mission: Turn Tracker Core into public-facing exposés: confirmed facts, alleged lanes, open questions, missing files, source routes, records, institutions, money routes, and next pulls.

Boundary: The Black File Exposé Engine is a public-record presentation layer. It does not turn proximity, wealth, social connection, transport capacity, file-index listing, or map-node appearance into guilt. Every exposé separates confirmed records, allegations, open leads, missing records, and what is not proven.

## Exposés Created

### BFE-001 — THE EPSTEIN FILES: THE PROTECTION NETWORK

A public-record map of criminal core, source releases, court lanes, institutional risk, missing records and the shield of redaction.

Confirmed lanes include: DOJ Epstein Library, DOJ privacy/redaction warnings, Data Sets 1-12, consolidated Court Records, United States v. Epstein, United States v. Maxwell, USVI v. JPMorgan, FOIA categories, prior disclosures, and the January 30 2026 DOJ production statement.

Missing records include: first ten Epstein PDFs, first ten Maxwell PDFs, USVI v. JPMorgan PDFs, FOIA category reviews, prior disclosure reviews, and public denial/counter-source cards.

### BFE-002 — EPSTEIN BANKING LANE: THE COMPLIANCE QUESTION

A focused exposé lane for banking records, USVI v. JPMorgan, compliance duties, settlement language and what still needs file-level review.

Confirmed: DOJ has a court-record page for Government of the United States Virgin Islands v. JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., No. 1:22-cv-10904.

Not yet proven by this page: banking wrongdoing, settlement terms, compliance failure or individual knowledge. Those require file-level review and verified cards.

### BFE-003 — THE RELEASE MACHINE: MILLIONS OF PAGES, REDACTIONS AND THE PROBLEM OF FAKE MATERIAL

A transparency exposé on the release system itself: scale, redactions, gated files, unreliable search, FOIA lanes and the fake-submission warning.

Confirmed: DOJ warns about sensitive/non-public material, search unreliability, redactions, and fake or falsely submitted public-submission material. DOJ also states the production includes more than 3 million additional responsive pages, more than 2,000 videos, 180,000 images and nearly 3.5 million pages when combined with prior releases.

## Rule

The trackers are the engine. The Black File exposés are the public case files. Every claim must show what is confirmed, what is alleged, what is open, what is missing, and what is not proven.

Use `data/black-file-exposes.json` for full structured expose data.
