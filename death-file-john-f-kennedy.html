Why do conspiracy theories exist around this death?

Evidence-Based Conclusion

The current evidence baseline supports the documented cause and official account only to the degree described in the cited record. The surrounding conflict and consequences justify continued investigation, but they do not independently prove a hidden operation. Ballistic, documentary, and eyewitness evidence strongly ties Oswald to the shooting, while many popular claims rely on weak witnesses, retrospective interpretation, or cumulative anomaly rather than a direct operational chain.

Analytical Inference — Not Established Fact

Kennedy was managing Cold War confrontation, intelligence oversight, Cuba policy, organised-crime pressure, and civil-rights conflict at the moment of his assassination. The timing and aftermath can create a rational investigative hypothesis, especially where transparency, security, forensic, or institutional questions remain. That hypothesis is analysis, not established fact.
