## How the systems fit together

1. **Agenda and standards:** Multilateral bodies, regulators, industry groups and standards organisations define common objectives and technical requirements.
2. **Law, funding and procurement:** Governments translate selected objectives into law, budgets, tenders, licences or compliance duties.
3. **Infrastructure and vendor dependency:** Technology, consulting, payment, security and cloud vendors build the operating systems.
4. **Interoperability and shared identity:** Identity, payment, health, mobility, platform and public-service systems exchange credentials or status.
5. **Conditional access and enforcement:** Eligibility, accounts, licences, travel, benefits or payment access can be restricted across connected systems.
6. **Permanence and lock-in:** Contracts, data standards, sunk costs and emergency measures make reversal difficult without a single world state.

**Status:** speculative scenario — not a factual forecast

**Plausibility:** moderate-to-high under continuation of current signals

**Evidence needed:** Binding cross-jurisdiction implementation; Shared technical standards; Documented identity, payment or eligibility exchange

**Would weaken it:** Durable decentralisation; Independent technical exit; Effective local veto and appeal
