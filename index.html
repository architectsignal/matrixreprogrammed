THE ACCOUNTABILITY TWIN

Open all consequence contracts

Every consequential action receives a Public Consequence Contract: the original source, authority and benefit still to be verified, money and beneficiary questions, falsifiers, and dated 30-, 90- and 365-day outcome checks. No verdict is allowed before the terms are locked.
