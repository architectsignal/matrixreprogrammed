WHO HOLDS POWER?

The site reads power as a network of roles, institutions, money routes, legal venues, policy channels, families, think tanks and media systems.

Evidence boundary: This page maps public relevance and investigation routes. It does not turn proximity into proof.

The visible stack

  • People: office holders, founders, investors, executives, advisers, media figures and policy actors.
  • Institutions: governments, regulators, banks, foundations, standard setters, agencies and international bodies.
  • Finance: asset managers, payment rails, central banks, funds, foundations and capital allocation.
  • Jurisdictions: courts, registries, tax systems, treaty seats, arbitration venues and corporate law centres.
  • Policy: digital ID, currency, health security, climate systems, data governance, security and speech rules.
  • Narrative: platforms, media, expert networks, social pressure and public framing.
