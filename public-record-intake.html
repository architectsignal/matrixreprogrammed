Evidence Boundary

A source lane does not prove a claim. It tells the system where to look. Every output must keep proven records, charges, documented associations, conflicts, allegations, hypotheses and unsupported claims separate.

Machine Flow

1. Pull

Collect source metadata from the correct lane: filing, docket, contract, rule, registry, award, sanction, project or news signal.

2. Grade

Apply the evidence ladder before the site displays a conclusion: proven, charged, documented, conflict, allegation, hypothesis, unsupported, or signal only.

3. Connect

Attach people, institutions, companies, contractors, agencies and money routes to the Power Entity Engine.

4. Brief

Push repeated patterns into Daily Brain, Outcome Briefings, risk clocks, Search V2 and the Evidence Vault.
