Open question

Why do conspiracy theories exist around this death?

The detailed conspiracy theory argues that Western powers supported regime change not only for civilian protection but to prevent African monetary, oil, and geopolitical independence, including an alleged gold-backed currency project. Supporters cite a Sidney Blumenthal memo alleging Libyan gold was intended for a Pan-African currency, France's economic interests, rapid rebel financial institutions, oil contracts, and the shift from protection to regime change.
