- `/follow-the-money`

- `/downloads/wealth-guides/<guide>.pdf`

## Evidence boundary

The Top 100 is a dated 1 March 2026 annual baseline. Net worth is an estimate, not salary, liquid cash or a claim of current exact wealth. Relationships and ownership structures do not establish misconduct.

The Making Money system is educational, not personalised investment, legal or tax advice. Future-capital sections are scenario analysis and include failure risks.

## Generated outputs

The Money Intelligence Pages workflow creates and validates 100 permanent wealth profiles and 16 branded PDF guides after source changes reach `main`.
