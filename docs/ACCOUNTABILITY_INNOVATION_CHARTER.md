# Matrix Reprogrammed Accountability Innovation Charter

## Permanent mission

> Matrix Reprogrammed is a public accountability system where anyone can search a consequence, trace it backwards through decisions, authority and money, follow the unanswered questions, and return to see what actually happened.

**Public promise:** Search the consequence. Trace the power. Follow the outcome.

This charter is a permanent product requirement. It must guide homepage design, search, dossiers, intelligence ingestion, human review, member follows, alerts, reports and future development.

## Locked systems

1. **Reverse Accountability Search** — begin with a consequence or lived problem and trace backwards to implementation, decisions, authority, money and unresolved questions.
2. **Power Supply Chain** — map who proposed, drafted, promoted, authorised, funded, implemented and benefited, while distinguishing authority from access and association.
3. **Evidence Half-Life** — re-verify evidence over time and issue recall notices when records become stale, unavailable or contradicted.
4. **Power Diff** — show exactly what was added, changed, ended, disputed or corrected since the previous version.
5. **Red-Team Mirror** — publish the strongest evidence-based case supporting and challenging each significant conclusion.
6. **Public Answer Clock** — keep a precise public-interest question, delivery history and response status attached to the permanent record.
7. **Missing Record Missions** — convert evidence gaps into structured, actionable public research missions.
8. **Lived Consequence Receipts** — accept privacy-protected evidence of real-world effects while separating individual reports from general proof.

## Current functional foundations

### Reverse Accountability Search

The first build searches current Accountability Twins and public-source leads. Each match must preserve a backwards path through consequence, implementation, authority, money, stated justification and outcome review. Search relevance is a route into evidence, never proof of causation, responsibility, benefit or wrongdoing.

### Power Supply Chain

Every reverse-search record receives a responsibility chain with these separate stages:

1. Observed consequence
2. Proposal
3. Drafting and design
4. Promotion and influence
5. Authorization
6. Funding and contracts
7. Implementation
8. Benefit, cost and risk transfer
9. Stated justification
10. Measured outcome

Unknown stages must remain visibly unresolved and classified as missing records. A meeting, friendship, donation, shared board, network link or documented access must not be displayed as decision authority unless a separate record proves the relevant legal, contractual, financial or delegated role.

## Operating boundary

The system must distinguish documented fact, official allegation, documented association, analytical inference, unverified claim and speculation. Proximity is never guilt. Every significant conclusion must explain what the evidence establishes, what it does not establish, credible alternatives and what evidence would change the judgement.

Automated systems may detect, organise, compare and propose. Public outcome judgements require accepted evidence and named human review. Corrections, counter-evidence, right of reply and version history remain attached to the record.
