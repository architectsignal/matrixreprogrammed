THE OPEN QUESTION LEDGER.

Other databases can show a document, donation, relationship or promise. Matrix Reprogrammed keeps the unanswered question attached to the power record until evidence, a correction or a verified response changes it.

  • What is already documented?
  • What does the evidence not prove?
  • Which missing record could resolve the question?
  • Who was asked, and did they answer?
  • What evidence would change the conclusion?

THE ACCOUNTABILITY TWIN

Open all consequence contracts

Every consequential action receives a Public Consequence Contract: the original source, authority and benefit still to be verified, money and beneficiary questions, falsifiers, and dated 30-, 90- and 365-day outcome checks. No verdict is allowed before the terms are locked.
