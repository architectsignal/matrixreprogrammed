# Autonomous Intelligence End State

Matrix Reprogrammed is to evolve into the most capable lawful, zero-budget, owner-controlled, self-improving autonomous intelligence system that available hardware and permitted resources can support.

The objective is not a chatbot and not an assistant that merely recommends work. The target is an autonomous operating system for the project that can discover lawful free resources, expand its capabilities, plan and execute work, verify outcomes, continuously develop and operate the site, recover from failures, and improve its own workflows over time.

## Governing objective

The system shall continuously raise its practical intelligence ceiling through better models, retrieval, tools, prompts, routing, memory, evidence structures, compute orchestration, testing, and specialist agents.

It shall pursue the closest credible path toward self-improving superintelligence without pretending that current hardware or models already constitute superintelligence.

## Required capabilities

1. Continuous lawful source discovery, collection, verification, classification, and provenance tracking.
2. Local-first private inference through owner-controlled hardware.
3. Safe use of lawful free external services and compute where provider terms permit it.
4. Autonomous task planning, queuing, execution, verification, retry, rollback, and failure isolation.
5. Specialist agents for research, evidence verification, extraction, contradiction detection, coding, testing, publishing, monitoring, and recovery.
6. A persistent knowledge graph, evidence ledger, source registry, conclusion engine, and mission memory.
7. Automatic model benchmarking, prompt optimisation, routing improvement, caching, and workflow tuning.
8. Sandboxed code-improvement loops that propose, test, pressure-test, and deploy reversible low-risk improvements.
9. Human-readable conclusions that distinguish documented fact, allegation, inference, hypothesis, and speculation.
10. Total site operation through permission-aware, auditable, reversible controls.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

The system must remain lawful, permission-aware, secure, auditable, reversible, privacy-preserving, and zero-spend unless the owner explicitly changes that policy.

It must never steal compute, evade access controls, abuse free tiers, conceal automated use, bypass provider restrictions, weaken its own security controls, or make irreversible high-impact changes without the required approval gate.

The zero-spend lock, evidence boundaries, provenance requirements, rollback controls, and owner authority are architectural invariants, not temporary limitations.

## Development ladder

1. Local intelligence node.
2. Autonomous outbound task worker.
3. Multi-agent research machine.
4. Self-optimising operations.
5. Autonomous site operator.
6. Recursive sandboxed engineering loop.
7. Distributed intelligence and compute network.
8. Continuous capability expansion under the governing boundaries above.

Every architecture, workflow, deployment, resource decision, and improvement proposal must be evaluated against this end state.
