Boundary

This is a public route map. It links records and trackers without treating association as a conclusion.

Map conclusions

The map is layered so readers can separate history, institutions, capital, execution, rails and evidence.

Asset-manager routes connect into the capital layer through holdings, voting, custody, mandates and filings.

Families and orders stay in the research layer unless records connect them to current institutions, offices, foundations or companies.

Contractor routes belong in the execution layer and should be checked through awards, services, executives and legal records.

Record gaps are prompts, not proof.
