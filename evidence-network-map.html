Evidence boundary

Select an entity or relationship

Entity nodes show their source footprint. Relationship lines show the source, date, grade, status, extraction method, what the record establishes and what it does not establish.

To find a documented route, select an entity, set it as the path start, then select another entity as the path end.

How to read the map

A LINE IS A CLAIM WITH LIMITS.

Core relationships

The default view excludes weak textual mentions and requires Grade A–C evidence with a confidence score of at least 0.60.

Textual mentions

Dashed lines mean only that a cited document contains the name. They do not establish ownership, payment, control, coordination or guilt.

Documented paths

Path highlighting finds the shortest currently visible route. It does not prove that the endpoints coordinated or even knew one another.

Corrections

Open the relationship record and primary source before drawing a conclusion. Submit replacement records or corrections through the public contact route.

Open-source notice: Cytoscape.js powers the public graph. Matrix Reprogrammed's evidence policy governs every conclusion and limitation.
