Evidence Queue

EVIDENCE HUNTER.

This page turns open claims and weak areas into evidence tasks. The aim is to prove, qualify, downgrade, or discard claims by finding public records.

Evidence boundary: An evidence task is not a conclusion. It is a request for records.

Priority tasks

  1. Attach people to institutions: official role pages, board records, filings, biographies, foundation pages.
  2. Trace policy to implementation: legal texts, public consultations, vendor contracts, regulator guidance, roadmaps.
  3. Trace reports to policy: think tank reports, funder disclosures, citations in law, meeting calendars.
  4. Trace jurisdiction routes: registries, court filings, tax rules, treaty texts, ownership records.
  5. Review open questions: find the primary source or mark the claim as unresolved.

Evidence grade

  • Grade 1 — lead or claim only
  • Grade 2 — reliable report with source trail
  • Grade 3 — official page, filing, annual report or public record
  • Grade 4 — court, regulator, treaty, procurement or implementation record
  • Grade 5 — multiple independent primary records
