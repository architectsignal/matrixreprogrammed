The non-negotiable boundary

WE INVESTIGATE RECORDS. WE DO NOT TARGET PEOPLE.

The service is limited to lawful public-record and open-source research. It does not obtain passwords, private messages, medical records, live locations, home addresses, intimate material or information acquired through impersonation, intrusion or coercion.

Payment never buys a preferred conclusion. A commissioned investigation may confirm, weaken, complicate or disprove the client’s starting theory.

What the deliverable means

A MAP OF THE RECORD. NOT A MANUFACTURED VERDICT.

Established

What reliable records directly support.

Alleged

What a named source asserts but does not independently establish.

Inferred

What the evidence reasonably suggests, with the reasoning shown.

Unresolved

What remains unknown, contradictory or unavailable.
