Card Rules

One card, one image

The visual card is a complete individual asset, not a text overlay or cropped screenshot.

Data stays live

Scores and links update in the dossier layer, so the artwork remains stable and playable.

Clean deck wall

The deck page shows the card image first with small buttons below for dossier and download.

Dossier behind every card

Each card opens an information page with score, timeline, source routes and submission form.

Art Direction

Museum-grade esoteric playing-card artwork, engraved linework, dark parchment, gold details, sharp border, readable hierarchy, no visual clutter, no broken typography.

Target structure: corner rank, suit marker, central portrait/seal, symbolic background, evidence-boundary ribbon, full-card download-safe composition.

Production rule: build artwork in batches of ten and verify every download before moving to the next batch.
