# Living Intelligence Delivery Scope

This branch repairs three production-facing systems:

1. Homepage actor intelligence must identify named people, companies, courts, regulators, institutions, laws, filings and documented actions rather than generic action labels.
2. Mission timer cards remain compact until the reader opens one deeper-information dropdown.
3. A verified Daily Control Brief subscriber receives the current brief immediately, with same-day idempotency and truthful provider status.

## Evidence and safety boundary

The site may expose documented corruption, censorship, coercive systems, criminal networks and institutional abuse through lawful public records. It must not convert association into guilt, publish private targeting information, or present speculative forecasts as established fact.
