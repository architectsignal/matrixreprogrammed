Editorial and Technical Method

HOW THE DEATH FILES WORK.

A living archive that separates official findings, evidence, inference, dispute, speculation, and disproven claims.

Three-Layer Conclusion System

Evidence-Based Conclusion

The strongest conclusion supported by currently available evidence and counter-evidence.

Analytical Inference

A reasoned interpretation, labelled as analysis with assumptions and alternatives.

Speculation

A testable but unproven scenario that can never overwrite the evidence conclusion.

Automatic Update Process

  1. Existing intelligence pipelines update public site datasets.
  2. The Death Files builder scans canonical names, aliases, organisations, events and keywords.
  3. Matches enter dossiers as leads, not findings.
  4. Dossier pages scan the current live-intelligence feed when opened.
  5. Every production rebuild regenerates runtime matches and timestamps.

The Five-Part Conspiracy Test

  1. Reason: explain why suspicion arose and identify the documented conflict.
  2. Suspected motive: state the proposed interest threatened by the person.
  3. Supporting clues: identify the facts and anomalies cited by theory supporters.
  4. Counter-evidence: present the strongest ordinary explanation and evidence weakening the theory.
  5. Proof required: state what authenticated evidence would confirm or falsify the causal claim.

Community Intelligence Rules

Signal Drops are public user submissions. Illegal material, doxxing, threats, protected victim identities, and unsupported accusations are prohibited.

THE DEATH FILES — evidence first, conclusion second.

Inclusion does not imply murder, conspiracy, or wrongdoing by any connected person. Official findings, established evidence, disputed claims, analytical inference, and speculation are displayed separately.
