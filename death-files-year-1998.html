Meyer claimed his technology could split water efficiently enough to power a vehicle and was pursuing investors and demonstrations. His death became one of the most cited suppressed-invention narratives.

1998 · Political leader · Custodial death

M.K.O. Abiola

Abiola remained the central symbol of Nigeria's annulled democratic election and was expected to be released during a political transition. His death removed the strongest claimant to the presidency and accelerated a managed transition.
