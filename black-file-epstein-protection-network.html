Deep Read

The protection question

Epstein is not exposed by counting names alone. The deeper question is how a criminal case became a long-lived institutional problem: who had records, who had duties, what was redacted, what was sealed, what was delayed, what was released, what remains gated, and which institutions had formal obligations instead of mere social proximity.

The evidence ladder

Lowest level: a name appears in an index or source map. Higher level: a public record confirms contact, money, travel, access or institutional action. Stronger level: a court filing, exhibit, regulator action, settlement, plea, conviction, order or official finding confirms the specific point. The page does not jump levels.

The missing-record problem

The most important material may be hidden not by absence, but by redaction, gating, bad OCR, handwritten documents, protective orders, sealed records, privacy restrictions, or millions of pages that nobody has classified yet. The site treats missing records as pressure points.
