Agenda 2030 / global-governance route

Track grants, policy influence, education, health, media, research and NGO funding routes.

Sources to check

Grant databases, annual reports, tax filings where available, charity registries.

Evidence class

Public-record lane

Update discipline

Weekly by default; faster during official releases, elections, filings, court events, crisis or sanctions changes.

Conclusion

  • Foundation support is a funding route, not proof of secret control without records.

Boundary

Agenda 2030 / SDG / global-governance alignment is tracked as public-record policy alignment, not as proof of wrongdoing by itself.
