Aviation Evidence Policy

HISTORICAL TRANSPORT RECORDS ONLY.

Matrix Reprogrammed can cite released flight logs, court exhibits, registry context and published transport records. It does not publish a live movement board for individuals.

Tracker DashboardPower MethodEpstein FilesSource Vault
AVIATION EVIDENCE RULES > Historical records only > Court exhibits over screenshots > Registry context over rumours > Separate owner, operator, charter and passenger claim > Do not publish live personal movement > Do not publish private addresses or family details

Why This Exists

Transport can matter as evidence when it appears in a file, exhibit, flight log, testimony or reputable report. The page exists to explain evidence standards, not to stalk anyone.

Transport Evidence Card Fields

Owner / operator / charter

Separate these fields. A registry owner does not prove passenger identity, and a charter reference does not prove ownership.

Historical date range

Use released logs or exhibits. No live or near-real-time personal movement monitoring.

Source class

Court exhibit, official registry, public report, company filing, open lead, rejected lead or privacy-blocked record.

Privacy flag

Mark sensitive data, missing context, blocked data and details that should not be published.
