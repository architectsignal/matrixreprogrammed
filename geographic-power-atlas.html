PRECISION IS PART OF THE EVIDENCE.

Public campus: a publicly documented institutional campus, with uncertainty kept within the stated radius.
City-level: the institution is represented by its publicly stated city or area, not a sensitive entrance or building coordinate.
Jurisdiction-level: the point represents a broad port, region or operating jurisdiction rather than a single facility.

WHAT THE MAP DOES NOT SHOW.

Nearby points are not evidence of collaboration. A source-domain count is not evidence that staff, money or decisions moved through a headquarters. The map does not expose private addresses, personal homes, covert sites, operational routes or inferred locations.
