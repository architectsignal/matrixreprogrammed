CARD BATCH TRACKER.

A simple production board for building the card artwork in clean batches of ten while preserving stable download and dossier routes.

Batch Plan

Batch 01

Cards 1–10

Goal: replace placeholders with final styled cards.

Verify: deck wall, dossier link, download link, health manifest.

Batch 02

Cards 11–20

Goal: preserve the same frame and symbol language.

Verify: no overlay text, no broken asset, no mismatched route.

Batch 03

Cards 21–30

Goal: complete middle sequence of the deck.

Verify: downloadable SVG now, PNG/PDF export later.

Batch 04

Cards 31–40

Goal: continue consistent card language.

Verify: profile carries live score and forum intake.

Batch 05

Cards 41–50

Goal: finish the final large batch.

Verify: manifest reports no missing artwork for these cards.

Final

Cards 51–52

Goal: complete the deck and run full visual QA.

Verify: card downloads, deep dossiers, health dashboard and homepage links.

Quality Gate

Playable

Every card must work as a standalone playing card image.

Downloadable

Every card needs a direct asset link and clean file route.

Linked

Every card must open its deep dossier without dead ends.

Consistent

Every batch uses the same dimensions, frame, typography hierarchy and visual rules.
