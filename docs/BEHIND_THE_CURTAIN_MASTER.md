# BEHIND THE CURTAIN — Permanent Master Requirement

## Mission

**Behind the Curtain** is Matrix Reprogrammed's highest structural-power synthesis layer. It must determine who or what possesses the durable capacity to shape outcomes without relying on fame, electoral office or personal celebrity.

The system follows:

- ownership and beneficial control;
- voting, board, trustee and appointment rights;
- capital allocation and crisis liquidity;
- payment, settlement, custody and market-access rails;
- infrastructure, technology and supply-chain chokepoints;
- legal, regulatory, standards and sanctions authority;
- intelligence, security and coercive capacity;
- information distribution and data access;
- institutional continuity across leadership changes;
- documented outcomes, dependencies, vetoes and constraints.

It must never begin with a predetermined cabal. It must test centralised, fragmented, competing-bloc, aligned-incentive and ordinary-institutional explanations and must publish evidence that weakens the site's own theories.

## Permanent analytical layers

1. Public faces
2. Operators
3. Gatekeepers
4. Owners and beneficiaries
5. Rule-makers
6. Continuity networks

Visibility is not control. Wealth is not command. Custody is not beneficial ownership. Association is not guilt. Benefit is not causation. Secrecy is not proof of wrongdoing.

## Structural Power Score

The score is an analytic model out of 100, never a statement of proven total control.

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Capital allocation power | 15 |
| Institutional reach | 15 |
| Infrastructure and gatekeeping control | 15 |
| Policy and legal influence | 10 |
| Appointment and succession influence | 10 |
| Information, data and narrative reach | 10 |
| Security or coercive capacity | 10 |
| Network centrality | 10 |
| Durability across leadership changes | 5 |

Every score requires sources, confidence, limitations, a counterargument, a replacement test, veto analysis, dependencies and evidence that could raise or lower the score.

## Classification system

Every material claim and relationship must be one of:

- `documented_fact`
- `official_allegation`
- `strongly_supported_assessment`
- `plausible_structural_inference`
- `speculative_hypothesis`
- `disputed`
- `unsupported`
- `rejected`

Users must be able to switch between Evidence Mode, Analysis Mode and Behind the Curtain Mode. Speculative links must be removable from the map.

## Final Top 10

The master list ranks the ten strongest **structural power centers**, not merely individuals. Eligible entries include people, state systems, financial networks, institutions, family offices, infrastructure gatekeepers, security networks and cross-institutional structures.

No candidate enters solely because of fame, net worth, title, symbolism, private-club membership or unsupported allegation. The ranking is provisional and continuously recalculated as reviewed evidence changes.

Every entry must show:

- current and previous rank;
- score and dimension breakdown;
- evidence confidence;
- visibility, opacity, replaceability, veto capacity and permanence;
- mechanisms, chokepoints and dependencies;
- who can constrain it;
- strongest evidence and strongest counterargument;
- evidence that would raise, lower or remove it;
- source ledger and last-reviewed date.

Historical snapshots must be preserved when future infrastructure supports them.

## The Access Layer — families, dynasties and continuity structures

The final conclusion above the Top 10 must ask who can legally or operationally reach the controls of each power center.

The Access Layer must separately measure:

- hereditary constitutional office;
- controlling voting rights;
- beneficial ownership;
- board and trustee representation;
- appointment and succession power;
- family-office and foundation capital;
- access to sovereign funds and state companies;
- company-level infrastructure control;
- historical institution-building;
- professional, advisory and political access;
- constraints, vetoes and external dependencies.

A family may be ranked only where public records document an actual mechanism. A surname, marriage, donation, social connection, club membership, historic reputation or symbolic association is never enough.

The family investigation must maintain three distinct lanes:

1. **Hereditary state authority** — ruling dynasties whose constitutional or legal powers are explicit.
2. **Corporate continuity control** — families whose voting shares, trusts, foundations or holding companies preserve documented control of major institutions.
3. **Historical architects** — families that materially helped build banking, sovereign credit, philanthropy, medicine, industry or policy networks but whose present-day command must be separately proven.

Every family entry must show:

- the precise legal or corporate mechanism;
- structures reached;
- score and evidence confidence;
- formal authority versus informal access;
- constraints and dependencies;
- strongest counterargument;
- claims the evidence does not support;
- source ledger;
- what would raise, lower or remove the family from the index.

The Access Layer must test all ten structural power centers and state who formally controls each one. Where no family control is documented, it must say so directly.

The Access Layer's permanent conclusion is evidence-led: families matter most where succession, ownership or voting power is legally embedded. Modern monetary, security, regulatory, technological and financial systems are otherwise governed by overlapping offices, boards, member states, regulators, executives, fiduciaries and professional networks. Evidence of access is not evidence of unified command.

## Continuous evidence feed

Relevant additions to the site are evaluated through:

1. entity resolution;
2. source authentication and classification;
3. relationship extraction;
4. mechanism-of-power classification;
5. outcome, veto, dependency and replacement tests;
6. contrary-evidence search;
7. score recalculation;
8. ranking simulation;
9. editorial review for sensitive claims;
10. change-log and network-map update.

Automated score changes require an entry in `data/behind-the-curtain-source-intake.json` with `reviewStatus: "approved"`, an existing `entityId`, an allowed classification, an HTTPS source and bounded score adjustments. Raw mention counts never change scores automatically.

## Red-team and sensitive-claim rules

Before publication, every conclusion must test correlation, custodial versus operational ownership, ceremonial relationships, normal professional overlap, institutional independence, competing actors, contrary outcomes, simpler explanations and source quality.

Never treat an ethnicity, religion, nationality, family or culture as one coordinated actor. Never infer crime from association. Serious allegations require editorial review. Private information unrelated to public-interest analysis must not be published.

## Current starting conclusion

The initial model does **not** establish one unified hidden command structure. It provisionally identifies competing state systems and overlapping monetary, legal, security, technological, settlement, standards and capital-allocation chokepoints. The family Access Layer finds the strongest direct control where hereditary law or corporate voting structures preserve authority. That conclusion must change if stronger evidence requires it.

## Production boundary

The Behind the Curtain workflow is Git-only. It may rebuild data, page assets and internal links, but it must not contain Wrangler, Cloudflare deployment or production-dispatch commands while the repository production freeze is active.
