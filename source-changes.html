Registered public-record sources are compared over time. Meaningful additions, removals, failures and restorations are recorded with retrieval dates and SHA-256 hashes.

Evidence boundary: A source change, failure or restoration is a transparency event, not automatic evidence of wrongdoing, concealment or intent.
