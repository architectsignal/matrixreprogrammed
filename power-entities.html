How to use the entity system

1. Identify the subject

Use the entity registry to find the canonical record, aliases, topic tags and linked public routes.

2. Inspect the relationship

Open the relationship registry to see whether a link is documented, inferred, disputed or explicitly speculative.

3. Verify the evidence

Follow the source route, publication date, evidence grade, correction record and missing-evidence requirement.

4. Test the conclusion

Compare the stated mechanism with counter-evidence and the next record capable of confirming or falsifying it.
