User-Friendly Intelligence Conclusion

How this evidence fits the mission

What the evidence shows

Nassar used medical and sporting authority to abuse patients and athletes over many years while institutions failed to stop him promptly.

Investigative lanes

child safeguardingmedical authoritysports governancelaw-enforcement failure

Why it matters

The case shows how professional expertise and institutional prestige can create repeated private access and suppress doubt.

Mechanism

Medical trust, treatment privacy, elite-sport hierarchy, fragmented complaints and delayed law-enforcement action.

Effect on the lane

Strongly strengthens authority-access and institutional-failure lanes.

What it points toward

Points toward mandatory cross-institution reporting, independent chaperoning and accountability for delayed referrals.

Strongest alternative explanation

Not every delay was intentional concealment; some reflected poor procedures, disbelief or jurisdictional confusion.

What it does not prove

It does not prove every coach, administrator, physician or agent knew of the abuse.
