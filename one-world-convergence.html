ONE WORLD CONVERGENCE MAP.

This page tracks where separate public systems appear to move toward greater coordination: identity, money, data, health, climate, speech, security and jurisdictional rules.

Evidence boundary: Convergence is an analysis lane. It needs source records before becoming a stronger conclusion.

Main lanes

  • Digital identity, credentials and access systems
  • Payment rails, CBDC research, stablecoins and programmable finance
  • AI, data governance and platform rules
  • Health-security coordination and emergency frameworks
  • Climate, energy and compliance reporting
  • Speech, information integrity and narrative governance
  • Security, sanctions and cross-border enforcement
  • Jurisdiction, tax, registry and treaty routes

What would prove stronger claims?

Specific laws, implementation roadmaps, procurement records, vendor contracts, regulator guidance, treaty texts, standards documents and opt-out rules.
