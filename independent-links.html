Evidence Rule

Start with the original record.

Use these sites to find documents, names, dates and claims. Then open the filing, judgment, dataset, study or first-hand record that carries the claim.

Comparison Rule

Read support and contradiction.

Strong research records what a source establishes, what it does not establish, and what credible evidence challenges its interpretation.

Correction Rule

Links can change.

Organisations close, rebrand and revise material. Report a dead link, inaccurate description or stronger replacement through the source-suggestion route.
