Submission Boundary

Evidence before accusation

Provide the original source, exact date, jurisdiction and what the record actually says. Separate documented fact from inference, allegation, speculation and personal opinion.

Do not submit: private victim identities, illegal material, passwords, hacked data, doxxing, threats or unsupported claims presented as established fact.

SIGNAL DROP CHECKLIST > Original source URL or document title > Publication / filing date > Person, institution or investigation lane > Exact claim supported > What the source does not prove > Contrary evidence or response > Why the lead matters > Evidence to seek next

What Happens Next

A lead should be checked against primary records, archived with provenance, entity-resolved and classified by legal and evidentiary status. Material changes should update the relevant dossier, timeline, relationship map, clock reassessment and user-friendly conclusion.

Publication is not automatic. Sensitive claims about living people require editorial review, accurate legal posture, right of reply, counter-evidence and a clear statement of what is not proven.

Public-record first. Protect victims. Preserve provenance. Association is not guilt. Charges are not convictions.
