Evidence Rule

No unsupported accusations. No private victim names. No screenshots as proof. No rumor framed as fact. Every item must either link to a source or be marked clearly as not verified.

CONFIRMED = official / court / verified source DEVELOPING = credible reporting, still moving ESTIMATE = modelled or NGO estimate REPORTED = claim logged, not necessarily verified COMPENSATED = claim accepted by scheme or court MIGRATION ENCOUNTER = border/contact statistic, not unique persons ASYLUM CLAIM = application statistic, not illegal-entry count NOT VERIFIED = claim seen, not supported CAUSATION WARNING = report does not prove cause

Migration / Irregular Immigration Flow Panel

Tracks migration, irregular or unauthorized border movement, and crime-data source splits without pretending every dataset measures the same thing. A border encounter, asylum application, removal, return, visa-overstay estimate, unauthorized-population estimate, and illegal-entry estimate are different categories. Encounters are not unique people. Nationality is not immigration status. Police suspects are not convictions. Total sexual-offence statistics exist in official national datasets, but the immigration-status split is usually separate, partial, or unavailable.
