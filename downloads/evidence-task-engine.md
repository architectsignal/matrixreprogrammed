# Evidence Task Engine

Updated: 2026-07-02

Boundary: This is an investigation workflow, not an accusation system. A task records what public source to pull next, what the source can prove, what it cannot prove, and how the result changes confidence.

## Statuses

- open
- pulled
- verified
- rejected
- needs counter-source
- privacy blocked

## Confidence Grades

- A: confirmed by primary public record, court record, official disclosure, regulatory filing, or authenticated exhibit
- B: strong lead supported by reputable reporting or multiple records but missing a primary record
- C: open lead requiring verification
- X: rejected, falsified, or speculation quarantined
