Evidence Classification

LEVEL A

Primary documented evidence

Authenticated original records, official documents, court material, verified media, or direct statements.

LEVEL B

Strong independent reporting

Multiple credible reports, established investigations, academic work, or verified testimony.

LEVEL C

Corroborated circumstantial evidence

Confirmed attendance, relationships, financial links, shared organizations, or recurring activity that does not by itself prove the stronger allegation.

LEVEL D

Unverified allegation

Anonymous claims, unsupported testimony, unauthenticated screenshots, or claims without original documentation.

LEVEL E

Symbolic interpretation

Clothing, colors, gestures, numerology, artistic imagery, mythology, or visual resemblance.

LEVEL F

Unsupported or refuted

Manipulated media, false quotations, wrong identities, incorrect dates, disproven stories, or claims with no traceable source.

Case-File Standard

Break viral collages and compilations into individual cases. Record the original source, publication date, person or organization, location, alleged meaning, documented circumstances, public response, supporting evidence, contradicting evidence, confidence grade, editorial status, and last review date.

Do not create a membership list from symbols, clothes, injuries, gestures, attendance, numerology, or association alone.

Evidence Drop

Submit verifiable evidence, original sources, corrections, or alternative explanations. Do not submit threats, private addresses, stolen credentials, illegal material, private victim identities, or unsupported accusations.
