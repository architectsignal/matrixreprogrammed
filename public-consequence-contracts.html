A future-facing accountability system

THE ACCOUNTABILITY TWIN.

A Public Consequence Contract remembers what power did, why it was justified, who may benefit, what evidence would prove it worked, what would falsify the case and exactly when the outcome must be checked again.

Evidence boundary: A contract is an accountability framework, not a legal contract, accusation or prediction. Lead-stage contracts cannot receive an outcome verdict until a primary decision record locks the terms.
Open JSON ledgerDownload CSVSubmit a decision record
1

Lock the original terms

Attach the primary decision, authority, stated rationale and measurable public benefit before any verdict is allowed.

2

Map who gains and who pays

Trace contracts, grants, ownership, institutional advantage, public cost and affected groups without assuming wrongdoing.

3

Check the real outcome

Reopen the record at 30, 90 and 365 days and compare measurable results with the original justification and falsifiers.
