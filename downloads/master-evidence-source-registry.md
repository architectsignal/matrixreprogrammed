# Master Evidence Source Registry

Generated: 2026-07-15T06:30:50.917Z

Boundary: This registry lists priority public-record and document lanes. It is not a claim that every source has already been ingested. Each lane is a route for verification, contradiction checks and missing-record upgrades.

## Government records
Primary source lane for laws, contracts, official reports, hearings, sanctions, procurement and agency records.

Examples:
- SEC EDGAR filings
- USAspending and SAM.gov contract records
- Federal Register and legislation records
- Treasury / OFAC sanctions releases
- Justice Department and court records
- UK Companies House and charity registers
- EU transparency and EUR-Lex records
- UN / WHO / IMF / World Bank / BIS public documents

## Court and legal records
Upgrade claims through complaints, judgments, indictments, settlement records, docket entries and sworn exhibits.

Examples:
- federal and state court dockets
- court exhibits
- settlement agreements
- deposition references
- criminal indictments
- regulatory enforcement actions

## Finance and ownership records
Track money, voting power, mandates, holdings, proxy votes, funds, custody and beneficial ownership.

Examples:
- 13F holdings
- 13D / 13G beneficial ownership
- N-PX proxy voting
- Form ADV
- 10-K / 10-Q annual and quarterly reports
- pension mandate records
- central-bank and BIS publications

## Contractor records
Track operational power through awards, vendors, services, executives, legal cases, task orders and public money.

Examples:
- procurement awards
- agency vendor lists
- defense and security contracts
- consulting awards
- logistics and intelligence-support records
- audit reports

## Media and wire-source lane
Use major media for discovery, timelines and corroboration, but upgrade only with primary records where possible.

Examples:
- Reuters
- Associated Press
- BBC
- Financial Times
- Wall Street Journal
- major court and finance reporting
- investigative outlets with documents

## Document archive / WikiLeaks-style lane
Use document dumps as leads requiring authentication, context, cross-checks and primary-record confirmation.

Examples:
- published document sets
- emails and cables where verifiable
- metadata checks
- cross-referenced official records
- redaction/context review

## Epstein files and related court record lane
Treat Epstein-related records as court/source lanes: docket documents, depositions, exhibits, official releases and named-record trails, not rumor.

Examples:
- court dockets
- released exhibits
- official document releases
- deposition references
- USVI / bank litigation records where public
- flight-log and address-book claims only with source boundaries

## Missing-record queue
The most important daily research tool. Every strong claim should name the next record that would prove, weaken or falsify it.

Examples:
- unreleased contracts
- unparsed filings
- sealed or missing exhibits
- uncited claims
- board overlap tables
- proxy-vote summaries
