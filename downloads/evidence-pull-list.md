# Top 50 Evidence Pull List

Updated: 2026-07-02

Boundary: This is a research task list, not a claim list. Pulling a record does not prove wrongdoing. Each record must be classified by what it shows, what it does not show, date, source, chain of custody and counter-source.

## Method

1. Start with the source route, not the theory.
2. Record the exact document title, date, author, parties and archive URL.
3. Classify the evidence: official disclosure, court record, regulatory filing, financial filing, foundation filing, oversight record, public archive, reputable reporting, open lead or speculation quarantined.
4. State what the record proves and what it cannot prove.
5. Feed the result into people, institution, money, Epstein, speculation or transport tracker cards.
6. Add confidence grade: A confirmed, B strong lead, C open lead, X rejected/speculation.
